HOW TO RUN:
1. Extract this ZIP folder anywhere (e.g. your Desktop or Documents).
2. Double-click "run.bat"
3. That's it! Your browser will open the live dashboard at http://localhost:8000.

DO I NEED PYTHON PRE-INSTALLED?
No! If you don't have Python, "run.bat" automatically detects that,
downloads official Python silently from python.org, sets up the
libraries, and launches the app in under 60 seconds.

DO I NEED NODE.JS, NPM, OR ANTIGRAVITY?
No! AutoHunter AI is 100% pre-compiled and standalone. It runs on any
Windows PC without installing node_modules or extra developer tools.

WHAT'S INCLUDED:
- 🎯 Global Freelance Radar: Live gigs from Discord, LinkedIn, RemoteOK, WWR, HN
- 📬 Gmail Outreach CRM: Track Sent, Pending, Approved, and Denied deals
- 🔄 2-Hour Autonomous Auto-Sync: Automatically scans feeds every 2 hours
- 🛡️ Anti-Scam Shield Bot: Analyzes client replies to prevent unpaid work & scams
- ⚡ 1-Click Cold Pitch Composer: Custom high-converting pitches that bypass AI detectors
- 📦 1-Click Deliverable Generator: AI builds the working code and deliverables for clients
- 📱 Android / Mobile Support: Access on your phone over Wi-Fi!

Enjoy hunting and closing deals!
